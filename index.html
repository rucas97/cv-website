02 / Research Focus

The Dark Genome at 6q23.

TARGET LOCUS

chr6:137,943,079 - 137,957,657 (hg38). Located 59 kb from the rs6920220 T1D risk variant.

EXPRESSION

SIMALR induced in 19/19 primary human islet donors. Absent in Type 2 Diabetic islets.

HLA BINDING

23 predicted strong binders. Four DQ8 and one DRB1*03:01 core confirmed.

CELL TYPE

Macrophage restricted. Detectable in 727 of 376,996 resting PBMCs.

More than 60 non-HLA risk loci are associated with Type 1 Diabetes, but the causal transcripts remain hidden. We identified SIMALR, a macrophage-restricted lncRNA that is induced by cytokines in every donor tested and encodes micropeptides predicted to bind the T1D-associated HLA class II alleles DQ8 and DRB1*03:01 — with zero sequence similarity to any known beta-cell autoantigen.
