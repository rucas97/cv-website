SIMALR lncRNA at the TNFAIP3 risk locus

A T1D-specific macrophage lncRNA induced by cytokines in 19 of 19 primary human islet donors. Predicted micropeptides bind DQ8 and DRB1*03:01 with no mimicry of known β-cell autoantigens.

Software

GenomicsOps.

ACMG/AMP · Strength-aware · Offline

Reproducible variant classification.

A Windows desktop application built around a deterministic combining engine that implements the ACMG/AMP 2015 rules, ClinGen VCEP strength modifiers, and the Tavtigian points-based extension.

Every classification records the criteria, the combining rule, and a SHA-256 hash over the evidence—so any result can be reconstructed and defended.
