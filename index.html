01 / Biological Foundation

The Dark Genome.

More than 60 non-HLA risk loci are associated with Type 1 Diabetes, but the causal transcripts remain hidden. My research focuses on the 6q23 locus harboring TNFAIP3.

We identified SIMALR, a macrophage-restricted lncRNA that is induced by cytokines in 19 out of 19 human islet donors. It encodes micropeptides predicted to bind HLA class II alleles (DQ8 and DRB1*03:01) with zero sequence similarity to known beta-cell autoantigens.

> SIMALR LOCUS

chr6:137,943,079-137,957,657 (hg38). 59 kb from the rs6920220 risk variant.

> EXPRESSION

Induced in 19/19 donors. Absent in Type 2 Diabetic islets. Regulated by NF-kB and STAT3.

> HLA BINDING

23 predicted strong binders. 4 DQ8 and 1 DRB1*03:01 core confirmed by independent algorithm.

> CELL TYPE

Macrophage restricted. Detectable in 727 of 376,996 resting PBMCs.
