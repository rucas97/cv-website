02 / Research Focus

Psychological Stress & T1D Pathogenesis.

GUT ARM

Chronic stress induces a TLR4/NF-κB module (Reg3b +5.18, Reg3g +3.79) inversely coupled to tight-junction genes (Ocln, Cldn15).

ISLET ARM

LPS-treated NOD β-cells mount a TRIF-selective response (Cxcl10 +1.46, Irf7 +1.95) but fail to induce CCL2/3/4.

INTEGRATION

A 41-gene stress-hormone core sits at the apex of the human T1D islet signature (NES = -1.73, padj = 0.0054).

MECHANISMS RULED OUT

Monocyte stress transcriptome, thymic AIRE suppression, and antibiotic models failed cross-cohort replication.

This project tests whether psychological stress contributes to T1D pathogenesis across five mechanistic dimensions using public GEO data. The integrated model proposes: stress disrupts the colonic barrier, LPS reaches β-cells eliciting CXCL10 without CCL2, T-cells are recruited and release IL-1β/IFN-γ, which unlocks CCL2 for myeloid amplification.
