Research Focus

The foundation of autoimmune disease.

My work bridges the gap between wet-lab molecular biology and dry-lab computational analysis to understand the roots of autoimmunity.

Dark Genome & Non-coding RNA

Investigating how long non-coding RNAs (like SIMALR) at risk loci drive disease. We found that cytokines activate a hidden non-coding program in primary human islets.

Featured Software

GenomicsOps.

My Proudest Work

Reproducible clinical variant classification.

A Windows desktop application built around a deterministic combining engine implementing the ACMG/AMP 2015 rules. Every classification records the criteria, the combining rule, and a SHA-256 hash over the evidence—so any result can be reconstructed and defended. Fully offline.
